# Testing guide

Want to help? These are the things on the [roadmap](RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md) that only a person playing can check. Pick
any of them. For each one: play that part, compare it with what's below, and if something's off, use **Report a Bug**
(title screen or Esc) right there. It fills in where you are.

You don't need to do them all, or in order. Even "I checked X and it was fine" is useful: say so in a report.

---

### 1. Waking up: Kreia and the medbay
**Where:** the very start of a new game.
- [ ] Kreia gets up off the slab in a scene, not by the camera jumping between you and her.
- [ ] Her voice plays with the conversation text.
- [ ] The medbay floor and walls aren't patchy or much darker than the rest of the room.

### 2. Atton
**Where:** the first conversations with Atton over the comm, then when he joins you.
- [ ] He walks and runs; he doesn't slide across the floor without moving his legs.
- [ ] His voice lines up with the text, and lines aren't missing.
- [ ] He blinks, and his face doesn't look frozen.

### 3. Playing as T3-M4
**Where:** the part of Peragus where you play as T3-M4, the droid.
- [ ] Walking and running feel right for a small droid (not too fast, not floaty).
- [ ] Shooting works and hits what you aim at.
- [ ] The camera sits lower for T3 and doesn't clip into walls.
- [ ] Opening the locked footlocker and using computer spikes on terminals works.

### 4. Fighting
**Where:** any fight, especially the mining droids and the HK-50 ambush.
- [ ] Enemy numbers feel like the original (not crowds, not empty rooms).
- [ ] You level up at about the pace you'd expect.
- [ ] Force powers look and sound right, and their cost makes sense.
- [ ] Companions fight on their own, and switching to them (V) works.

### 5. Voices and sound on the later levels
**Where:** the dormitories, the hangar, the Harbinger.
- [ ] Every line in a conversation has its voice (note any that are silent).
- [ ] Music and ambient sound play in every area.

### 6. Lighting and floors
**Where:** everywhere; especially the medbay, the mining tunnels and the Harbinger.
- [ ] No black or dark patches on floors that shouldn't be there.
- [ ] No rooms that are far too bright or too dark compared with the original.

### 7. Workbench, lab station and upgrades
**Where:** any workbench or lab station on Peragus.
- [ ] Opening it shows the right items, and upgrading works.
- [ ] Prices and part costs look sensible.

### 8. Character creation
**Where:** New Game.
- [ ] Male and female characters, every class and several faces look right in the game, not only in the menu.

### 9. Saving and loading
**Where:** anywhere, after a fight and after opening doors.
- [ ] Save, quit the game completely, open it again and load: dead enemies stay dead, open doors stay open, your
      items and party are the same.

### 10. Mods (optional)
**Where:** title screen → Mods.
- [ ] Switching the HD texture mods off and on (then Restart to apply) swaps between standard and sharp textures.
- [ ] Saving a collection and switching to it brings back the same mods.

---

**Things already known** are in [KNOWN_ISSUES.md](KNOWN_ISSUES.md): no need to report those, unless you find out
more. Thank you for testing!
