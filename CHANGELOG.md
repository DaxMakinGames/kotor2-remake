# Changelog

What changed in each version, newest first. Every release's full notes, with downloads, are on the
[releases page](https://github.com/DaxMakinGames/kotor2-remake/releases).

## 0.9.12 (2026-11-23)

- **A Force wheel you choose.** The wheel holds six powers, so a Jedi with dozens still picks from a clear ring. A new
  Powers page (O, or between Character and Party) lists every power you know by side, with its cost and description,
  and the wheel as it looks in play: pick a power to put it on or take it off, and move it round with [ and ]. Passive
  powers work by being known and take no place. Until you choose, the wheel holds your strongest powers.
- **What you wear shows.** Every suit of armour and every weapon in the game can now be seen worn and held, not only
  the ones found on the levels built so far, so armour bought at a store changes how you look. Companions change too:
  Atton, Mira, Visas, Bao-Dur and the Disciple wear the armour you give them, as in the original. Fixed: changing
  armour with a lightsaber in hand left your character standing in the air, out of sight.
- **A better inventory.** Filter by kind (weapons, armor, usable, gear, upgrades, junk) and sort by name, value or
  newest. Items that beat what you're wearing say BETTER, and "Equip better" puts them all on; it only suggests what the
  original's rules would have you wear well (armour you're trained in, and none that would shut off a Jedi's powers).
  Mark items as junk with J, right-click or Y, and sell all your junk at once from a store's Sell tab.
- **Every action on a controller.** Hold L3 for Stealth Mode and R3 to have the party hold or follow (a tap still
  switches character and sends the party at your target). In the inventory, LT and RT change the filter, R3 the sort
  and X equips better gear.
- **People stand on the ground.** With Effects on Enhanced, everyone has a soft shadow on the floor beneath them. It
  follows slopes and stairs, fades as they leave the ground, and goes with them when they're hidden.

## 0.9.11 (2026-11-23)

**Fixed**
- **Screens no longer wash out white.** Glowing screens and displays were drawn lit by the room *and* glowing on top,
  and Peragus's big star-map displays summed to a white sheet over the window. Screens now glow as the original's do,
  never brighter than their own picture.
- **Setup works on SteamOS.** The Steam Deck's own build of KOTOR 2 names its folders in lower case, and Setup stopped at
  the game data stage on the first level of Peragus. Setup now reads the game's folders however they're spelled.
- **Lighting in Enhanced mode:** rooms, props and people without baked light kept no ambient light at all in the largest
  rooms. They keep the level's own light now.

**New**
- **Setup fits your screen.** It opens at a size to suit the display it's on, sharp on a 1440p, 4K or Retina screen,
  and can be resized.
- **Enhanced Force powers.** Lightning forks and crackles over its target; Push, Whirlwind, Wave and Scream bend the air;
  Drain pulls the life back to you; Choke and Fear leave a dark mist; Stasis and Stun a frozen shimmer; buffs keep a
  soft aura while they last.
- **Enhanced ambience.** Steam and gas are soft where they meet walls, and lit by the room on High; sparking cables
  flicker light; a little dust hangs in the air on High.
- **Menus checked** on ultrawide (21:9), 4K and Steam Deck screens.
- **The original's own conversations** that the story starts from the game's shared files now play: the party's
  reactions and Peragus's console readouts among them.

## 0.9.10 (2026-11-20)
- **Skills in play:** the levels' own mines (found with Awareness, disarmed or recovered with Demolitions), mine kits
  you can set, and Stealth Mode.
- **Combat balance:** enemies scale with the party, as KOTOR 2's own rules have them.
- **The party talks:** every companion's own battle cries, pain, low health and more, at the moments the game had them.
- **The world:** a livelier Citadel Station, the original's movies at their moments, and lighting fixed where the
  original's was broken.
- **Mods:** conversations, quests and stores of a mod's own; classic KOTOR 2 Override mods (textures and models); four
  new example mods; the modding wiki online.
